# M4 measurements

Produced by `scripts/m4-comparator-research-gate.mjs` against `main` at
`44cb82db7365e436c642f375852f22990c45a6ce`. The machine-readable form is
`evidence.json`. Everything here is **Observed**; proposals live in
`architecture.md` and verdicts in `decision-matrix.md`.

The compositing measured is the **production** `src/utils/pdfDiff.ts`, driven in
a real browser. Measuring a rewrite of it would say nothing about what ships.

## 0. The corpus

31 synthetic drawings, generated by `scripts/m4-comparator-fixtures.mjs` and not
committed. The same plan — border, title block, repeating grid, a labelled title
— drawn as a fraction of whatever sheet it is given, so "the same drawing on A1
and A3" means the same picture at two physical scales.

actually differ makes every number below meaningless:

## 4b. A plan, then a verdict

The first version of this prototype returned `CHANGE` as soon as a comparison
was eligible, so `CHANGE` was doing two jobs and an identical drawing came back
as one. A plan says whether the comparison can be made; only a comparison that
actually ran may say MATCH or CHANGE.

| | plan | verdict | ink differing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| identical | `READY_TO_COMPARE` | **MATCH** | 0.0% |
| a wall added | `READY_TO_COMPARE` | **CHANGE** | 9.6% |
| rotation only | `READY_TO_COMPARE` | **MATCH** | 0.0% |
| crop origin only | `READY_TO_COMPARE` | **MATCH** | 0.0% |
| a different sheet | `GEOMETRY_MISMATCH` | **none** | not compared |
| a different sheet, human alignment supplied | `READY_TO_COMPARE` | still to be earned | — |

Match tolerance 0.5% of ink, because rendering is not bit-exact and antialiasing
puts a handful of pixels either side of every line. Without it MATCH would be
unreachable.

The rotation and crop rows reach MATCH only because the plan renders upright;
that is what "the plan is ready" means for those pairs.

## 4c. More than two members

The shipped rule marks a pixel matched when **any other layer** has ink near it.
With two members that is the same as agreement. With four it is not.

| | any-other-layer (shipped) | reference-pairs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| four identical | 0.0% **MATCH** | MATCH |
| three the same, one changed | 9.6% **CHANGE** | CHANGE |
| **two against two** | **0.0% MATCH** | **CHANGE** |
| **reference and three different** | **0.0% MATCH** | **CHANGE** |

The last two rows are the finding. In *two against two*, A and B put a wall in
one place and C and D put it in another; every pixel finds a partner and the
comparison comes back **perfectly clean**. In *reference and three different*,
three documents each differ from the reference and from each other, and it is
still reported as a match.

Comparing each non-reference member against slot 1 catches both: the two-against-
two pairs measure `identical-a4: 0.0%`, `wall-at-y-a4: 19.3%`,
`wall-at-y-a4-copy: 19.3%`, and the overall verdict is MATCH only when every
pair matches.

| plan | members | contract | status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| two members | 2 | any | `READY_TO_COMPARE` |
| four members | 4 | two-only | `UNSUPPORTED` |
| four members | 4 | reference-pairs | `READY_TO_COMPARE`, 3 pairs |
| one member | 1 | any | `UNSUPPORTED` |

## 4e. Three code paths, three answers

Preview, the full export and the change report each compute their own render
scale, and only two of them cap it. Arithmetic only — nothing here was
allocated.

| sheet | zoom / dpi | preview | export | change report | report asks for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A4 | 1 / 300 | 4.17 | 4.17 | 4.17 | 8.7 Mpx, 244 MB |
| A4 | 2 / 300 | 8.33 | **4.17** | 8.33 | 34.8 Mpx, 975 MB |
| A4 | 6 / 600 | 12.63 (capped) | 8.33 | **50** | **1253 Mpx, 35 GB** |
| A1 | 1 / 300 | 4.17 | 4.17 | 4.17 | 69.7 Mpx, 1951 MB |
| A1 | 2 / 300 | 4.46 (capped) | 4.17 | 8.33 | 278.8 Mpx, 7805 MB |
| A1 | 6 / 600 | 4.46 (capped) | 4.46 (capped) | **50** | **10035 Mpx, 281 GB** |

**The change report has no cap at all.** `PdfComparator.tsx:416` computes
`scale * (dpi / 72)` and there is no `MAX_DIM` or `MAX_AREA` anywhere in that
function, so it asks for whatever the zoom and DPI multiply to. The export would
have capped the same request to 4.46.

They also disagree about a missing page:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| preview | skips the member (`PdfComparator.tsx:173`) |
| export | skips the member, still adds the page (`:316`) |
| change report | **skips the whole page** when fewer than two members render (`:437`) |

Three paths, three behaviours, one user asking one question.

## 8. What counts as ink

`computeMultiPdfComposite` uses the **mean** of the channels
(`pdfDiff.ts:57-60`). `detectChangeBounds` uses **any** channel
(`pdfDiff.ts:190-193`).

| | rgb | composite | change bounds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| black | 0,0,0 | ink | ink |
| dark grey | 64,64,64 | ink | ink |
| mid grey | 140,140,140 | ink | ink |
| CAD cyan | 0,158,217 | ink | ink |
| CAD yellow | 255,225,0 | ink | ink |
| **pale yellow** | **255,255,140** | **not ink** | **ink** |
| pale grey | 210,210,210 | not ink | not ink |
| near white | 245,245,245 | not ink | not ink |
| white | 255,255,255 | not ink | not ink |

CAD yellow is ink to both — mean 160, blue channel 0 — which is why it is not the
case that exposes the difference. A pale yellow is.

| | canvas | total | composite | working set |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A4 150 dpi, threshold 0 | 1240x1753 | 88 ms | 25 ms | 61 MB |
| A4 150 dpi, threshold 2 | 1240x1753 | — | 48 ms | 61 MB |
| A4 150 dpi, threshold 4 | 1240x1753 | — | 55 ms | 61 MB |
| A4 300 dpi, threshold 0 | 2480x3507 | — | 98 ms | 244 MB |
| A4 150 dpi, 4 layers | 1240x1753 | — | — | 122 MB |
| A3 150 dpi, threshold 0 | 1753x2480 | 183 ms | 73 ms | 122 MB |
| A1 150 dpi, threshold 0 | 3507x4966 | 628 ms | 251 ms | 488 MB |

A neighbourhood threshold costs more than an exact one, and run-to-run variance
on a 30–50 ms workload is large enough that the multiple is not stable: measured
between 1.3x and 2.2x across runs at radius 2. It does **not** scale with the
area of the search box — radius 4 is a 3.2x larger box than radius 2 and cost
about the same — because `hasNeighborInAny` returns as soon as it finds ink.

That early exit is what section 9b removes.

## 9b. The threshold when nothing matches

Two sheets with no shared border, title block or grid, so almost no ink matches
and the neighbourhood search runs to completion on nearly every ink pixel. 0.5 mm
converted to a pixel radius per resolution.

| | radius | canvas | ink | change | composite |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| matching, A4 150 dpi | 0 | 1240x1753 | 3.7% | 0.0% | 31 ms |
| matching, A4 150 dpi | 3 | 1240x1753 | 3.7% | 0.0% | 61 ms |
| **not matching**, A4 150 dpi | 0 | 1240x1753 | 1.9% | **100.0%** | 46 ms |
| **not matching**, A4 150 dpi | 3 | 1240x1753 | 1.9% | **100.0%** | 43 ms |
| **not matching**, A4 300 dpi | 0 | 2480x3507 | 1.7% | **100.0%** | 150 ms |
| **not matching**, A4 300 dpi | 6 | 2480x3507 | 1.7% | **100.0%** | 207 ms |

Wall-clock totals mislead here: the adversarial pair is the *sparser* drawing, so
comparing page times would credit it for having less to do. **Per ink pixel** is
the measure that isolates the effect:

| | per ink pixel |
| --- | --- |
| matching, 150 dpi, radius 3 | **0.59 us** |
| not matching, 150 dpi, radius 3 | **1.56 us** |

**2.6x**, and that is the number a work bound has to be set against. Every
figure in section 9 was taken on matching drawings and is therefore a floor.

A physical threshold makes this worse rather than better at higher resolution,
because the radius grows with the DPI: 43 ms at 150 dpi with radius 3 against
207 ms at 300 dpi with radius 6, on the same drawing.

## 13. The gate

`scripts/m4-comparator-research-gate.mjs` re-asserts **93 claims, 37 of them
negative probes**. Several are unusual: the shipped comparator is asserted to
report changes on drawings that are identical. Those are the findings, and a gate
in which the baseline passed everything would prove nothing about why this spike
exists.
