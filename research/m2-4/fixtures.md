# M2-4 synthetic corpus

22 PDFs and 22 answer keys, generated by `scripts/research-m2-4-fixtures.mjs`. **No customer document and no real project drawing is used anywhere.** Every page is drawn from text we control with the OFL font already shipped for OCR (`public/ocr/fonts/MPLUS1p-Regular.ttf`), and nothing generated is committed — `test-fixtures/` is ignored.

Regenerate with `node scripts/research-m2-4-fixtures.mjs`. Two runs produce **44/44 byte-identical files**: pdf-lib metadata is pinned to the epoch and the speckle generator is a fixed-seed mulberry32, so a measured difference is a property of the algorithm and never of the run.

## Answer keys

Each `<name>.pdf` has a `<name>.truth.json` beside it, in **display space at scale 1** — origin top-left, y downwards, PDF points. That is the space the probes measure in, so no coordinate flip sits between the answer and the measurement. For the `/Rotate 90` fixture the key is mapped into the rotated space the viewer shows, rather than left for the probe to reconcile.

A key records, per table: the grid size, the outer box, every column and row boundary, and every cell with its text, its span and its rectangle. Cell-level truth is what makes "12 of 12 cells correct" a measurement instead of an impression.

## Adversarial cases — the correct answer is zero tables

This half is the point of the corpus. An architectural sheet is made of things that look like tables to a clustering algorithm.

| fixture | source | why it is a trap |
|---|---|---|
| `adv-title-block` | native | a ruled label/value box in the sheet corner, with a divider between label and value — **a closed ruled grid, structurally identical to a two-column schedule** |
| `adv-legend` | native | three aligned columns of symbol / Japanese / English |
| `adv-keynote-list` | native | numbered notes: two aligned columns, one of them prose |
| `adv-grid-dimensions` | native | a structural column grid is a **literal** grid of ruled lines, with dimension strings evenly spaced below it |
| `adv-room-labels` | native | room names and areas scattered over a plan, aligned by accident, plus a key plan |
| `adv-note-columns` | native | two independent columns of prose, Japanese beside English |
| `adv-full-sheet` | native | all of the above on one sheet, with a drawing border, and no schedule anywhere on it |
| `adv-scanned-sheet` | scanned | the same trap with only OCR boxes to judge it from |

A false positive on any of these is a **high-severity** result: the user is handed a spreadsheet of drawing furniture that looks exactly as authoritative as a correct one.

## What the corpus does not cover

Stated so the measurements are not read as broader than they are.

- Real scanner artefacts: JPEG ringing, uneven illumination, bleed-through, dust along a fold. The speckle model is isolated dark pixels and small clumps.
- Handwriting, stamps, redlines.
- Tables continuing across a page break.
- Rotated or vertical **text** inside a cell (the rotated *page* is covered).
- Dense CAD line-work of the kind a real plan carries — the drawings here are schematic, so the ruling-line counts are lower than a real sheet would produce.
- Non-Japanese CJK, and right-to-left scripts.
